**For:** Sahil Mittal and the acquisition/onboarding team · **Date:** 2026-10-09 · **Status:** internal framework (recommendation). Not programme terms. Not legal or tax advice.

No cash, credits, points, commission reductions, Creator Advantage points, promotion slots or any other reward is offered for referring anyone. The ₹50/₹100 code values must not be mentioned.

## 1. Who should refer

Professionals whose work naturally connects them to other professionals, and who have had a good experience:

| Referrer | Why they are natural introducers | Who they would introduce |
|---|---|---|
| Wedding photographers and filmmakers | At every wedding with designers, MUAs, mehendi artists, emcees | Wedding & Occasion pod |
| Makeup studios and academies | Work with designers and photographers; train other artists | MUAs, mehendi, designers |
| Bridal and occasion labels | Work with stylists, jewellers, photographers | Jewellery, MUAs, photographers |
| Interior and architecture studios | Specify makers; commission photographers | Makers, architecture photographers |
| Furniture and décor makers | Supply many studios | Studios |
| Design studios | Serve young labels | Labels, illustrators |
| Emcees | Meet every vendor at events | Wedding vendors |

**Only activated professionals are asked**, and only those who show satisfaction: they completed set-up without unresolved complaints and engaged with the team.

## 2. Why they would refer (non-monetary motivations)

- **Their own ecosystem.** Having the people they already work with next to them makes Uepaki more useful to them and to their shared customers.
- **Professional generosity.** Recommending a peer they respect.
- **Shaping the category.** Founding professionals help set the standard of their category in their city.
- **Credibility by association**, kept honest: they choose who they would be comfortable appearing alongside.

Never appeal to guilt, obligation ("you owe us"), or invented future rewards.

## 3. When to ask

| Moment | Ask? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| At registration | **No** | No experience yet; feels transactional (and the Refer & Earn redirect must be removed) |
| At activation (first listing live) | **Soft ask, once** | They have just seen the set-up experience |
| After the founder feedback session or the 7-day check-in, if positive | **Yes** | Satisfaction is fresh |
| After their first genuine transaction (post-launch) | **Yes** | Strongest moment: real value |
| After a complaint, or while an issue is open | **No** | — |
| Repeatedly | **No** | At most one ask per stage; never more than three in total |

## 4. How to ask (scripts)

**Activation (soft ask).**

> Thank you for setting up with us. One question, with no obligation: are there two or three professionals you work with — a photographer, a makeup artist, a maker — who you think would be a good fit for Uepaki in [CITY]? If so, and you're comfortable with me mentioning you, I'd write to them personally. If you'd rather not, that's completely fine.

**After a positive experience or transaction.**

> I'm glad that went well. If there's anyone in your circle you think should hear about Uepaki, I'd be happy to write to them personally. I'd only mention your name if you're happy for me to.

**Writing to the introduced peer (only with the introducer's consent to be named).**

> Hello [NAME], [INTRODUCER NAME] suggested I write to you — they're one of the professionals setting up on Uepaki in [CITY], and they thought your [RELEVANT WORK] would fit well alongside theirs. [STANDARD SEGMENT M1 FROM THE MESSAGE LIBRARY, from the description onward.]

**If the introducer prefers not to be named:** contact the peer with the standard cold M1 (only through a verified business route). Do not say "someone recommended you".

### Data and consent rules

- The **introducer** gives consent to be named in the introduction. Record it with date and wording.
- The **peer's contact details** must be their **public business route** (website email, professional Instagram), or a route the peer has agreed to share. Do not use a personal phone number passed on by the introducer unless the peer has agreed. Lawyer review: DPDP Act, Q-LEGAL-OUT.
- Tell the introducer the outcome only if the peer agrees ("[Peer] has joined and is happy for you to know").
- Introduced peers go through the same outreach rules: 1:1, opt-out, no WhatsApp unless invited.

## 5. What can be offered

| Can offer | Notes |
|---|---|
| Thanks, personally from the founder | — |
| Telling the introducer when the peer joins | Only with the peer's consent |
| Priority **support** during the introducer's own onboarding questions | Operational; not a visibility benefit |
| Inclusion in the founder feedback sessions | Already part of the founding programme proposal; not conditional on referring |

Nothing that has monetary value, ranking or visibility effect, or that depends on how many people they refer.

## 6. What must not be promised

- Cash, vouchers, credits, discounts or commission reductions for referring (E4 deferred; DEC-012 pending; TDS questions open).
- The ₹50 / ₹100 figures present in code.
- Creator Advantage points, Promoter or Elite levels, or the social-promotion benefit, in exchange for referrals.
- Better ranking, featuring, or "more visibility" for referrers.
- That the referred peer will get customers.
- Leaderboards or competitions ("top referrers win…").

## 7. Future formal referral programme (only after founder decision)

Pre-conditions before any incentivised programme:

1. The founder lifts the E4 deferral and sets figures (DEC-012).
2. Legal review: terms, disclosure, data protection, anti-abuse.
3. CA review: TDS and GST treatment of rewards (CA-AFF-TDS).
4. Engineering: remove the legacy referral pages (E4); build tracking with fraud controls (no self-referral, duplicate-identity checks, rewards only after the referred seller is **activated**, not merely registered).
5. A policy check against B1/FD-03: no referral reward may take the form of ranking or placement.

Design recommendations for that future programme:

- Reward only on the referred seller's activation (or first genuine transaction), never on registration.
- Rewards in a form that does not touch visibility (for example, a fee credit, if the CA approves).
- Caps per referrer; transparent terms; disclosure that the referrer receives a reward when they recommend.
