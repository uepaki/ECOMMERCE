# Uepaki — "Who Has Joined?" Response Framework

**For:** everyone who speaks to professionals, and whoever approves public statements · **Date:** 2026-10-09 · **Status:** internal operating standard. Public statements still need founder sign-off (and the claims registry where it applies).

**The objection:** *"Who else has joined?"* It is the most important objection in a two-sided marketplace before launch. Established professionals don't want to be first and alone.

**The constraint:** answer it without:

- fabricating names;
- inflating numbers;
- presenting registrations as active professionals;
- implying endorsement.

Founder decision E3 requires real, consented people only. The registry records the legacy "487 of 500" counter as an invented figure, never to be repeated.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Live, defined numbers only.** Every number comes from the system on the day it is used, with a definition and an as-of date.
2. **Activated, not registered.** "Professionals on Uepaki" means **activated** professionals (approved, profile complete, at least one live listing, payout details complete). Registrations are reported separately, and labelled as such.
3. **Names only with recorded consent.** Consent is specific (to be named to peers, in social posts, in press), dated, and revocable.
4. **No implied endorsement.** A professional being on Uepaki is not an endorsement of Uepaki, unless they have given a quote in writing for that use. Membership of an association (FDCI, for example) or a stockist relationship is never used to imply that body's endorsement.
5. **Honesty about stage beats a big number.** At zero or near zero, the honest answer is the process: who is being invited, how, and why.
6. **Round down, never up.** "40+" only if 40 or more are live today. Below 10, use the exact number.

## 2. The data behind every answer: a Verified Professional Register

A single internal sheet, which the system or the onboarding lead updates daily:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Seller ID / Prospect ID | Link to the system and the outreach tracker |
| Professional / studio name (as they use it) | — |
| Category, segment, city | For category and city counts |
| Status | Registered / Approved / **Activated** / Paused / Left |
| Activation date | Counting and founding qualification |
| Consent: named to peers (Y/N, date, wording) | 1:1 answers |
| Consent: social posts (Y/N, date, wording) | Founding promotion; public showcase |
| Consent: press/PR (Y/N, date, wording) | PR strategy ("named founding creators who have agreed to speak") |
| Quote for public use (text, date, approval) | Testimonials, only once real and approved |
| Consent withdrawn (date) | Immediate removal everywhere |
| Test/staff account (Y/N) | Excluded from all counts |

**Counting rules**

- Exclude test, staff, duplicate and related accounts.
- A professional who leaves, pauses or is suspended is removed from "activated" counts the same day.
- City counts use the professional's verified city only.
- Counts are reported with an as-of date: "as of 14 November".

## 3. The answer ladder (what to say at each stage)

| Stage | Condition (recommendation) | What to say 1:1 | What may be published (after founder sign-off) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — Starting** | Fewer than 10 activated professionals in total | The honest stage, plus the process: "We're at the start. We're inviting a first group, city by city, across complementary categories. As of today, [N] have completed set-up." Exact N, even if 0–9 | Nothing numerical. Mission and process only, within approved claims |
| **1 — First pods** | 10 or more activated in total; at least one city with professionals in 3 or more categories | Live counts by category; consented names relevant to the person asking (same city or pod) | Optional: "Professionals across [categories] are setting up on Uepaki in [cities]" — no number, or the exact live number with an as-of date |
| **2 — Credible pods** | At least one complete pod (Operating System §11) in two or more cities; 50% or more of activated professionals consented to be named | Counts by city × category; 2–4 consented names from their pod; offer to introduce them to a founding professional (with that professional's agreement) | A live coverage summary (see §5); consented founding-professional posts on Uepaki's social channels (the founding promotion benefit, S07) |
| **3 — Post-launch** | Live customers | As stage 2, plus real activity statistics only if measured and approved | Coverage summary; real, consented professional stories (E3); aggregate statistics only with a cited source |

## 4. Scripts

**Stage 0**

> Honestly, we're at the start. As of today [N] professionals have completed set-up. We're building each city in small groups — a designer, a makeup artist, a photographer — so the first professionals are complementary rather than competing. That's why I'm writing before launch rather than after it.

**Stage 1–2**

> As of today, [N] professionals are set up on Uepaki, across [categories]. In [CITY], that includes [category list]. [NAME] and [NAME], who work in [their area], have said I can mention them. If it would help, I can ask whether one of them would be happy to have a short chat with you.

**If asked for a number you don't have yet**

> I'd rather give you the exact figure than a guess. As of today it's [N]. I'll send you an update when it changes.

**If asked "Is [famous name] on Uepaki?"**

> I can only tell you about professionals who've joined and agreed to be named. [If they haven't joined: I can't speak for anyone who hasn't.]

Never confirm or deny a conversation with anyone.

**If asked "Is [association/stockist/platform] involved?"**

> No. Professionals who join Uepaki do so individually. Nobody else is endorsing or partnering with us unless we say so explicitly and in writing.

## 5. What to publish once enough verified professionals exist

**A live "Professionals on Uepaki" coverage summary** (on the website or in founder updates, after founder sign-off):

- activated professionals by category, with the definition and as-of date;
- cities where at least one pod is complete;
- registered-but-not-yet-activated shown separately, or not shown at all.

**Founding professional features on Uepaki's social channels** — the founding promotion benefit:

- one professional per post;
- their real work, credited;
- with their written consent;
- following the rule-based rotation in the founding programme.

**Real professional stories** (E3), only after launch and with consent:

- documentary in tone (Brand Doctrine);
- no outcome claims unless measured and approved.

**Not published:**

- counters that tick upward, or "spots remaining" counters (unless live and approved);
- on-platform "featured founding members" placements — that would be a visibility advantage, which S07 rules out;
- logos of associations, stockists or platforms;
- unapproved quotes.

## 6. Approval and audit

| Item | Approver |
|---|---|
| Any public number | Founder, on the day, against the register |
| Any public name or quote | Founder, plus the professional's recorded consent |
| Any product statement in the same material | Claims registry (APPROVED — PUBLIC) |

Monthly audit, run by the onboarding lead and reviewed by the founder:

- every name used publicly has a matching consent entry;
- every number used in the month matched the register on its date;
- withdrawn consents were removed everywhere within 2 business days (test threshold).

## 7. What never to do

- Invent, estimate, round up or "project" member numbers.
- Present registrations as active professionals.
- Name anyone without consent, or anyone "in talks".
- Use a famous name, before they join and consent, to attract others.
- Use logos or names of associations, stockists or other platforms.
- Suggest that being on Uepaki means a professional endorses it.
- Create urgency from counts ("only 12 seats left") unless live and approved under FP-OUT.
