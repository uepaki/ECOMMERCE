# Uepaki — Outreach Tone and Voice Guide

**For:** anyone writing to a professional on Uepaki's behalf · **Date:** 2026-10-09 · **Status:** internal operating standard.
**Authority:** Brand Doctrine 01–05 (2026-10-06) for voice; Founder Decision Brief Rev 07 and the founder records of 6 October 2026 for what may be said. If this guide and those records disagree, the records win.

---

## 1. The one-sentence test

Every message should read like **a senior creative professional writing to a peer about something real they are building** — not a platform recruiting users.

If a message could be sent unchanged to 500 people, it is not ready to send to one.

## 2. Voice

| Trait | In practice | Not this |
|---|---|---|
| **Precise** | Name the specific piece of work you looked at. Describe Uepaki in one plain sentence. | "We love your amazing content." |
| **Calm** | Short sentences. One question. No urgency. | "Spots are filling fast." |
| **Warm without softness** | Respect for their time and craft; a real person signs. | Gushing praise, emojis, "dear creator". |
| **Ambitious without arrogance** | "We are building…" | "The first / best / only platform for…" |
| **Honest about stage** | "We are preparing to launch. We haven't opened to customers yet." | Implying traction, demand or a crowd. |
| **Invitational** | Invite to a conversation; respect a no. | "Join now", "Sign up today", "Don't miss out". |

Registers (Brand Voice §1): **Declarative** for the one-line description; **Architectural** when explaining how something works (only what is verified); **Human** for the opening observation and the close.

## 3. Structure of a good first message

1. **One specific, accurate observation** about their work (see the Personalization Guide).
2. **Who you are**, by name and role.
3. **What Uepaki is** — the founder-approved description (FD-06): *"Uepaki is a marketplace where customers can discover and buy products and connect with creative professionals for services, bookings and projects."*
4. **Why them, specifically** — the segment relevance line.
5. **Honest stage** — preparing to launch in India; inviting a first group city by city, across complementary categories.
6. **Founding group** — only in the founder-approved FP-OUT wording.
7. **One low-effort question.**
8. **An easy way out** — "If this isn't relevant, just say so and I won't write again."

## 4. Vocabulary

| Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|
| professional, practice, studio, label, maker | creator economy, influencer, user |
| clients, customers, collaborators | followers, audience, fans, reach |
| conversation, invitation, founding group | campaign, opportunity of a lifetime, exclusive offer |
| a professional profile / place for your work under your own name | storefront that drives sales, shop that sells itself |
| preparing to launch | coming soon (as hype), launching big |
| additional channel | replacement for, better than |
| "I'll send it in writing" | "trust me", "don't worry about the details" |

Indian English spelling (jewellery, colour, organise). Indian settings and cities. Money in ₹ with GST stated where relevant.

## 5. Channel calibration

| Channel | Tone | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email | Architectural + human | 120–220 words for M1 | Subject names their studio/label; no "Re:" tricks on first contact |
| Instagram DM | Human, brief | ≤ 600 characters for M1 (limit 1,000) | No links in the first DM; no voice notes cold |
| LinkedIn | Architectural, peer-to-peer | Connection note ≤ 200 characters | Use for practices, studios, production companies only |
| WhatsApp | Conversational, still restrained | Short | **Never cold.** Only after the professional invites it; opt-in recorded for any update messages |

## 6. Founder-led vs team-led

- **Founder-signed:** category and city anchors, tier C (strong mid-tier) professionals, architects, anyone who asks "who is behind this?", all premium/aspirational introductions (Wave 4).
- **Team-signed:** tier A/B independents in Waves 1–2, using the same text frame — always with a real name and role, never "Team Uepaki".
- Never write as the founder when the founder did not send or approve the message.

## 7. Emotional guardrails

- Never speculate about a professional's finances, workload, struggles or need for work ("desperate", "struggling", "need more clients").
- Never use a creator's difficulty as a hook. No clinical or medical framing of frustrations (Brand Voice §7.9).
- Never flatter. One accurate observation is more respectful than three compliments.
- Never guilt ("we expected to hear from you").
- Respect seasons: wedding professionals are busy in peak months; offer to come back later.

## 8. DO NOT SAY — the concise list

(Also kept as a sheet in `UEPAKI_OUTREACH_MESSAGE_MASTER_LIBRARY.xlsx` and as `UEPAKI_DO_NOT_SAY.md`.)

**Money and terms**
- "Free", "free to join", "free registration", "no cost" — registration on the website is ₹1 + GST (SUB-01).
- Any commission rate, range, or "lower than X".
- Plan prices; "70% off"; "early-bird" amounts; "off forever".
- Founding-programme numbers ("first 500", "lifetime", "2% lower commission") unless the founder has approved them for outreach (FP-OUT).
- The 2% social-promotion offer (terms open, D2–D9).

**Outcomes and visibility**
- Any promise of customers, clients, leads, enquiries, bookings, orders, sales or income.
- "Grow your business", "boost your reach", "get discovered", "more visibility".
- "Visibility cannot be bought", "equal visibility", "fair algorithm", "level playing field", "priority placement", "featured".
- Any explanation of ranking, Fair Reach or "how the algorithm works".

**Social proof and scarcity**
- Any number of members/users that is not live from the system.
- Names of anyone who has not joined and agreed to be named.
- "Top designers have joined", "leading names", "everyone is joining".
- "Limited spots", "filling fast", "last chance", deadlines that are not real and approved.

**Positioning**
- Names of other platforms, marketplaces or stockists; any comparison or criticism of them.
- "First", "only", "best", "fairest", "revolutionary", "game-changing", "disruptive", "seamless", "empowering".
- "Curated", "exclusive", "hand-picked", "only professional-grade" — Uepaki is open to all sellers (E10).
- "Influencer", "followers", "audience", "go viral", "monetise".

**Unlaunched or unconfirmed things**
- Fame Circle, Brand Ambassador, referral rewards, "refer and earn" (referral launch deferred, E4).
- Collaboration features — unless stated exactly as "Planned: [feature]. Not available yet." and listed by the founder (RD-1a).
- Specific launch features as live (bookings, chat, storefronts, analytics) before EV-09 confirms them.
- A launch date.
- Shipping partners, delivery times, returns promises, payout timing.
- Off-platform communication rules (SP-4a open).
- Company history, funding, team size, awards, user numbers.

**Tone**
- Exclamation marks. Emojis in first contact. "Dear creator". "Hey dear". All-caps.
- "Desperate", "struggling", "need work", or any comment on finances.
- "Opportunity", "don't miss", "act now", "join now".
